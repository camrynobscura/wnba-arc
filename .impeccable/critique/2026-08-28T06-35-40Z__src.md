## Priority Issues

### [P0] One legend, two meanings — ScaleKey collides with itself
ScaleKey noun="baseline" renders identically at CareerHeatmap.tsx:100 and SummaryView.tsx:150. In the heatmap "baseline" = the player's own career average; in the bars = league/position average. Two referents, one word, same scroll.
Why: comprehension core. A reader calibrated on the heatmap concludes the app contradicts itself when the bar disagrees.
Fix: pass the real referent as noun in each place; better, drop ScaleKey from the bars and give them an axis. Fix ScaleKey.tsx's stale "Season Breakdown" docstring.
Command: /impeccable clarify

NOTE — corrected claim: Assessment A alleged bar saturation ramps by position "regardless of magnitude." ### [P1] The bar has no scale; the caption that carried one was deleted

Compounding: the ruler is mixed by stat type (FULL_STEPS=3 for counting; relative-% ±50% for fgp/tpp/tsPct), unmarked, in one aligned column with a shared center rail.

Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Drill-down axis labels are unroundable; domain crushes percentage stats
deviation.ts:551 maxVal = max(...)*1.2; deviation.ts:649 axisTicks = [0,50,100].map(yPct => fmtV((maxVal*yPct)/100)). Ticks land at 0, 0.6*max, 1.2*max — never round. A'ja points (max 26.9) reads 0.0 / 16.1 / 32.3.
Domain is always zero-based: FG% 51.8 vs ~43 baseline in a 0-67 range = ~13% of the 220px plot (~29px); blocks 2.0 vs 0.7 gets ~42%.
Fix: snap domain to a nice interval and derive labels from snapped ticks; zero-based for counting stats, padded data-driven domain for percentages.
Command: /impeccable shape
